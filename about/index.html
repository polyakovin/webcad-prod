The project

About WebCAD

WebCAD is a set of browser tools for making parts, working with existing models, and preparing them for printing or sharing.

Explore the tools ↗

01 / The idea

Why separate tools?

If all you need is to drill a hole in an STL, you can go straight to Mesh Editor. It opens with a sample and puts the file picker and editing controls on the same page. When you need to design a part from a sketch, use Workspace.

Files connect the tools. Make a gear in Gear Generator, download its STL, then open it in Model Stand to record a turntable video. You can also take the same STL into your slicer.

The tools read your files on your device. Workspace saves its editable project in the current browser, and you can download a JSON copy to keep or move it.
